Production Incident Intelligence

Upload application logs and service metrics as CSV — every metric is checked against its own recent trailing baseline, and the same statistical test flags per-minute ERROR/CRITICAL log bursts. Signals within 10 minutes of each other are then correlated into incidents across services — a payments-service outage and the checkout-api cascade it causes a few minutes later show up as one incident, not two unrelated blips. Within each incident, services are ranked by which one started failing first — the standard first heuristic for finding a likely root cause before anyone's done a full investigation — and a per-service timeline shows the cascade shape at a glance. Every incident is also classified critical, warning, or info from its peak severity, duration, and whether it has spread across services — and can be acknowledged or resolved directly below, with a resolution note and mean-time-to-resolution tracked across everything that's been closed out. Sample data below (a payment-gateway outage that cascades into checkout) is auto-seeded so there's something to explore immediately.

Detection already runs automatically right after every upload — this just re-runs it on demand.
